6 Steps From Kickoff to Cutover.

Migrations are risky, so we work in phases that ease the burden on your team. We've moved dozens of automotive retailers across every size, complexity, and feature set - and we adjust the playbook to fit your situation.

01

Brand & Theme

We work with your existing brand or build new assets if you're refreshing. Either way, the result is a homepage design approved before anything else moves - header, footer, and key components styled to match.

02

Data

We map every data source - your current platform, supplier feeds, internal spreadsheets - into a single clean catalog. Our PIM handles the category tree, attribute rules, fitment, and validation so the import is right the first time.

03

Content

You hand off the content you already have - in-house imagery, about us, contact info, static pages, policies - using our Content Collection process. We apply it to the new site.

04

Testing

Our team runs internal QA on a full staging environment, then hands it to you for User Acceptance Testing. Anything broken gets fixed before we touch production.

05

Launch

We follow an 80+ step cutover checklist so nothing slips - payment, shipping, tax, redirects, DNS, integrations, and final settings all coordinated. Customer-facing downtime is zero.

06

Marketing & Optimization

Migrating is the easy part. Making the new site outperform the old one is the fun part. We can run your marketing, shape your strategy, or hand you the playbook and step back - whichever fits how your team operates.
