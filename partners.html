Questions

Common questions, answered.

Why an application instead of a "contact us" form?+−

Because we're not chasing logos. We take on a limited number of partners each quarter so the team we promised is the team you actually get. The application helps us route you to the right strategist before we ever meet.

What does pricing look like?+−

It depends on scope. We offer fixed-fee retainers, performance-tied models, and hybrid structures. Qualified partners doing real volume often choose performance tie-ins — we have no problem putting our fee on the line.

How long is the typical engagement?+−

Our average partner has been with us 3.4 years. We're built for compounding work, not quarterly sprints. That said, every engagement starts with a clear 90-day plan so you know exactly what early wins look like.

Do you work with our existing internal team?+−

Always. The best results come when we extend an in-house team rather than replace it. We'll scope the engagement around what your people already own and where we can add the most leverage.

What happens after I submit the application?+−

A senior strategist reviews it within two business days. If we see a fit, we'll send a calendar link for a 30-minute call — no qualification call with an SDR, no follow-up sequence. Just a real conversation about whether we can help.
