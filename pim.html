Auto Parts PIM to Simplify Your Life

Purpose-built for auto parts retailers that are burned out on stitching together spreadsheets, mis-matched fitment values, and managing multiple imports.

Book a Demo

Most PIMs weren't built for auto parts, and most auto parts PIMs suck...

PROBLEM 01

Garbage In, Garbage Out

Plug-n-Play automations and multiple data sources create a cluster of duplicate product info, mis-matched fitment values, and an overall bad shopping experience.

PROBLEM 02

Connector fees stack up fast.

Competitors charge $199–$299/mo extra just for the Shopify connector. Add a second store, a DAM, an import tool — your "$499 PIM" is suddenly $1,200/mo before you've enriched a single product.

PROBLEM 03

No Data Transformation

Most PIMs accept whatever you import, creating duplicates and bad data. The strict ones reject it and leave you cleaning up spreadsheets by hand. Neither option scales.

Take Control of Your Data & Catalog

Tired of being held hostage by fragmented tools and having to submit tickets every time you need to make a change? Your catalog should work for you, not against you. Own your data. Control your fitment. Run your business on your terms.

Feature 01

Centralized Catalog Management

One place for every product, every variant, every price. Purpose-built catalog architecture designed specifically for automotive parts, and fast even with tens of thousands of SKUs.

  • +Simple and configurable products, with every variant's price, cost and shipping in one panel
  • +Edit thousands of products like a spreadsheet: fill down, apply to all, change prices by %
  • +Every supplier price type kept per product and variant; you choose which one Shopify gets
  • +Scheduled sale prices that start and end by themselves
  • +Supplier runs never wipe a price, and a file that would blank prices warns first
  • +One filter panel on every list, and exports of exactly the rows you filtered or ticked
  • +Undo most changes, with an activity log of who changed what, and when
  • +Run several stores from one login, with the right access for each person
  • +A new interface with light and dark mode that works on your phone
Feature 02

Vehicle Fitment That Actually Fits

The only fitment engine designed for the messy reality of automotive catalogs. YMM is a first-class citizen — not a metafield.

  • +Unlimited custom columns: Year, Make, Model, Engine, Trim, Drivetrain, and beyond
  • +Approved vehicle lists that flag vehicles that don't exist, with suggested fixes that respect the row's years
  • +Strict, warn or open validation: warn holds questionable rows for review instead of importing bad values
  • +Merge look-alike spellings store-wide, with each one's vehicles shown first
  • +Per-variant fitment for tires and wheels, plus Universal and Hide Fitment per product
  • +Copy fitment from one SKU to many, shift years in bulk, and re-import only what changed
  • +VehicleID, BaseVehicleID and ACES files translated to Year, Make, Model, engine and submodel
  • +Inline edit, CSV, XLSX, and Google Sheets import with saved mapping profiles
  • +Validation reports and an export of every product with no fitment, ready to fill in
Feature 03

Data Hub: Supplier Data, Cleaned on Import

Feed tools pass along whatever suppliers send. The Data Hub brings supplier data in on your schedule and cleans it on the way in, so shoppers see one clean value instead of five versions of the same thing.

  • +Connectors for SEMA Data, ATD, Turn 14 and Western Power Sports, each on its own schedule
  • +Flat files, XML, APIs and database exports, picked up on a schedule from SFTP, FTP or S3
  • +Drag supplier fields onto yours, with a live preview of a real product
  • +Mismatched values matched: Blk, BLACK, blk → Black, with consistent bed lengths and engine names
  • +Choose the brands you carry and what each brings: products, prices, images, fitment, inventory
  • +Rules per source; saving is the only step, and every save can be restored
  • +Questions asked once, in Review, while good products keep moving
  • +Fitment tags built in the PIM and sent with the product push
  • +One report email per run, and only when something needs you
Feature 04

Native Shopify Integration

An embedded experience inside Shopify admin — not a thin wrapper. The Shopify connector is included, not a $299/mo add-on.

  • +Embedded app inside Shopify admin, with fitment right on the Shopify product page
  • +Products, images, prices, inventory, collections, redirects and fitment tags sent together
  • +Fitment sent to your YMM lookup, with only the columns each app needs
  • +Import your existing Shopify catalog in one pass
  • +Collection trees, bulk collection images, and a redirect manager that untangles chains
  • +Changes made in Shopify flow back on their own, and your edits are never overwritten
  • +Anything that doesn't reach Shopify is listed with the reason and retried automatically
  • +Plain-English warnings, with an incident ID and an email when each one is fixed
Feature 05

Product Options & Customization

Configurable add-ons, custom fields, and conditional logic. Uses Hulk Options format for compatibility with existing workflows.

  • +Dropdowns, radio buttons, swatches, color images, multi-selects, text fields
  • +Target by product, collection, or rule-based conditions
  • +Match on title, vendor, tag, SKU, price, weight, inventory
  • +Hulk-compatible import / export for easy migration
Feature 06

Public Fitment API

Power your storefront, mobile app, or third-party tools with clean, server-to-server fitment data.

  • +Full fitment exports, column list and catalog values
  • +SKU lookup and Year / Make / Model search
  • +Changes-since sync, so your apps pull only what changed
  • +Choose which fitment columns each YMM app receives
Feature 07

Import, Export & Data Transfer

Move catalog data in and out at any scale: by hand, on a schedule, or straight from your own systems.

  • +CSV, TSV, XLSX, XLS, ODS or a ZIP with images; Shopify and Matrixify files recognized
  • +Scheduled imports pick up the newest file from SFTP, FTP, FTPS or S3
  • +Feed API: push products, fitment, redirects and deletions from your ERP or database
  • +Saved, reusable mapping profiles with a preview and checks before anything changes
  • +Merge, Replace or Remove for tags, images and sales channels, with no hidden defaults
  • +One live progress panel for product, fitment, option and collection imports
  • +Quick export and saved Google Sheets profiles

How Your Data Flows

SEMA Data, Turn 14, Western Power Sports, ATD, flat files, XML, APIs, external APIs and databases all come in through one place. The Data Hub cleans and checks them, the PIM keeps one catalog, and Shopify gets the same clean data every time.
