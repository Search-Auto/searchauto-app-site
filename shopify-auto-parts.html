FAQ

Questions We Get About Moving to Shopify

Don't see yours? Ask us.

Will I lose my Google rankings when I move?

Not if it's done right. We map every old URL to its new one with 301 redirects, keep sitemap parity, carry over titles, meta, and structured data, and watch Search Console closely after launch.

Will my store go down during the switch?

No. We build and test everything on a staging site first, then follow an 80+ step cutover checklist on launch day. Customer-facing downtime is zero.

Can you bring over my fitment data?

Yes. Whether it lives in ACES files, spreadsheets, or your current platform, we move your Year/Make/Model data and clean it up on the way. Our PIM handles the category tree, attributes, and fitment validation so the import is right the first time.

Can you build a brand-new store, not just migrate?

Yes. We build new parts stores from scratch: theme, catalog, fitment from your supplier feeds or ACES files, checkout, shipping, and tax. Same process, minus moving an old site.

Do I need Shopify Plus?

Most parts stores don't on day one. Plus makes sense when you need wholesale/B2B pricing, checkout customization, or more scale. It's the same store, so upgrading later isn't another migration.

What does it cost?

It depends on your catalog, your data, and what needs to connect. Fill out the quote form and we'll come back with a scope and a price in writing.

Do you only build, or do you market the store too?

Both. Every migration comes with 30 days of post-launch monitoring. After that we can run your SEO, Google Ads, Meta, Amazon Ads, and email, or hand your team the playbook.
